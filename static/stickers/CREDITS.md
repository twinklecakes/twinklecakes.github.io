# Collage image sources

All are public domain, from Wikimedia Commons. The stickers were cut out with `tools/make_cutouts.py`.

- **Vampire squid**: Carl Chun, *Vampyroteuthis infernalis*, plate from *Die Cephalopoden* (1910).
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Vampyroteuthis_infernalis_Chun_1910.jpg
- **Grandfather clock**: "My grandfather's clock", *Popular Science Monthly* vol. 30 (1886–87), tinted to match the others.
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:PSM_V30_D664_My_grandfathers_clock.jpg
- **Red lily**: Pierre-Joseph Redouté, *Lilium chalcedonicum*, *Les Liliacées* (1802–1816).
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lilium_chalcedonicum_in_Les_liliacees.jpg
- **White lily**: Pierre-Joseph Redouté, *Lilium candidum*, *Les Liliacées* (1802–1816).
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lilium_candidum_in_Les_liliacees.jpg
